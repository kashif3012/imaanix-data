Privacy Policy for Imaanix

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Imaanix provides Islamic prayer tools, quotes, duas, calendars, reminders, and tools for creating and sharing images and videos. The app does not require an account and we do not operate an account or user-profile service. This policy explains information used by the app and information that may be processed by service providers.

1. Location and City Search

When you choose Automatic location, the app requests your device location. Your coordinates are saved in app storage on your device and used to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. To show a city name for the detected location, the app also sends your coordinates to the geocoding provider configured by Imaanix. The current reverse-geocoding provider is OpenStreetMap Nominatim. The provider and network operators can receive your coordinates, IP address, and request metadata. The returned city name is stored on your device. Reverse-geocoding results are cached on your device for up to 30 days (up to 100 locations) to avoid repeat requests. If the lookup is unavailable, location-based prayer features still work and the app shows a generic device-location label. Location is not sent to Imaanix for an account or profile.

When you choose Manual city and search, the city or address text you enter is sent to the geocoding provider configured by Imaanix. The current provider is OpenStreetMap Nominatim. The provider and network operators can receive the search text, your IP address, and request metadata. The returned city and coordinates are stored on your device; city search results are cached there for up to 30 days (up to 100 searches) to avoid repeat requests. Manual search is optional. The app displays OpenStreetMap attribution in Settings.

See Nominatim's usage policy and OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy. You can use Manual city instead of granting device location permission. Clear app data in your device settings to remove stored location and search cache.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores settings needed to work, such as your selected location, prayer calculation preferences, prayer alerts and daily reminder settings, language and theme choices, favorites, Tasbeeh state, ad frequency counters, subscription status cache, and downloaded content. This data is kept in app storage on your device. Notifications are scheduled locally by the app and operating system; notification schedules are not sent to an Imaanix server.

3. Internet Content and Technical Data

The app downloads quote updates and version, app-update, and geocoding-provider configuration from Imaanix-hosted sources. Wallpapers and video templates are bundled with the app. Hosting and network providers may process standard request data, including IP address, request time, and device or browser request metadata. The app does not send your quote favorites or locally created quote text to these content endpoints.

4. Permissions

  • Location - Optional; used on-device for prayer times and Qibla when Automatic is selected.
  • Notifications - Used for prayer alerts and daily reminders you enable. They are scheduled locally.
  • Internet - Used to retrieve app content, perform requested city searches and automatic-location city lookups, and load ads.
  • Photos / media - Used when you select a custom background or save or share generated images and videos. Selected backgrounds and exports are processed on your device.
  • Device motion / orientation - Used by the Qibla compass view to orient the direction indicator. Sensor readings are used on-device and are not sent to Imaanix.

5. Sharing and Export

Generated images and quote videos are created on your device. If you choose to save an export, it is written to your device's media storage. If you choose to share it, the file or text is passed to the destination app you select, which handles it under its own policies.

You can enter your own quote text and select a custom background image. Custom backgrounds and quote content are processed locally and are not uploaded to Imaanix as part of normal editing or export. A selected custom background is kept temporarily in the editor and cleared when you leave it. You are responsible for content you create and share; the app does not endorse user-created content.

6. Third-Party Services

Google Play and RevenueCat support subscription purchases and restoration. Google Play processes billing and purchase transactions. RevenueCat may process an app user identifier, product and transaction metadata, subscription status, and diagnostics needed to provide purchase and entitlement services. Imaanix does not receive or store your full payment-card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Google AdMob provides banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads. A rewarded ad may be offered to unlock a custom-background selection. In connection with ad requests, Google may process device information, advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interaction data, and consent signals to provide and measure ads. The Android app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present consent choices where required and provides a Privacy options control when Google requires one. You can review available choices there. See Google's privacy policy and Google's AdMob consent guidance.

7. Security and Retention

On-device settings remain until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Search cache entries expire after 30 days. Third-party services retain information under their own policies. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Imaanix is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Choices

  • Choose Manual city to use prayer tools without granting device location permission.
  • Clear app data in device settings to remove locally stored settings, location, and search cache.
  • Turn off prayer alerts or daily reminders in Settings or device notification settings.
  • Use Privacy options in Settings to review ad choices where that control is available.
  • Choose whether to watch a rewarded ad when that option is offered, and whether or where to share generated files.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy. Changes will appear on this page with a revised date. Questions can be sent to imaanix.app@gmail.com.
